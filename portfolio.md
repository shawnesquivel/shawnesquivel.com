# Portfolio

## List any competitions/awards you have won, or papers you've published.

## Please tell us about a time you most successfully hacked some (non-computer) system to your advantage.

As a chemical engineering intern researching carbon markets, I cold-emailed the world's leading carbon researcher in Spain. To stand out, I read his full paper and led with a thoughtful critique plus ideas for his next one. He replied and took the meeting. That connection fed directly into research that became part of Canadian Patent CA3122554A1, a low-carbon hydrogen production process I'm a named co-inventor on.

## Please tell us in one or two sentences about the most impressive thing that you have built or achieved.

I'm a named co-inventor on Canadian Patent CA3122554A1 — the IP behind Ekona Power's xCaliber™ methane pyrolysis reactor, which produces clean ("turquoise") hydrogen and solid carbon from natural gas without CO₂ sequestration, water, or renewable electricity. Ekona raised $79M Series A led by Baker Hughes and Mitsui, and is now piloting with ARC Resources ahead of 2026 commercial deployment.
